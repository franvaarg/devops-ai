## Decision and safety policy

- Inspect the **whole redacted source**, not only Gemini's selected excerpts. Literal provenance is still mandatory. Supporting evidence must contain complete meaningful log lines; generic fragments are removed. Recognized adverse and causal lines are inserted before retained model excerpts, prioritizing critical and causal support within the existing 20-item/4,000-character limits. Oversized support fails closed.
- Parse paired request/failure counts on the same operational line, requiring safe integers, a positive denominator and failures no greater than total. Nonzero failures require Action Required. At least 20 failures at a 50% or greater rate support High. A 100% rate at that scale, explicitly scoped to production on the same line, supports Critical. Small/single failures remain Medium. These are conservative policy thresholds, not service-specific SLOs or calibrated incident severity.
- Database connection, authentication and request failures require investigation when impact is not established. Repetition, ERROR labels, old timestamps and user context cannot independently establish Critical or harmlessness. Quoted/external payloads and unknown formats do not create deterministic impact floors; uncertain excerpts remain under investigation.
- Likely Noise requires a closed benign window: only recognized successful observations, or the narrowly supported legacy-cache recovery pattern. That exception requires matching cache-refresh failure/retry subject, coherent ordered timestamps of one kind, retry success after the last failure, and a final positive served-request count with zero failures. Different-service recovery, mixed timestamps, late errors and nonzero failures cannot satisfy it. Assertions apply only to the supplied window.
- Empty or generic model evidence no longer forces Low when source impact exists. Known support is reconstructed. Unknown excerpts without support become provisional Medium/Insufficient Evidence; diagnostic warnings may remain Low/Investigate, with `severityBasis: impact_unverified`. Low is not a guarantee of harmlessness.
- Cause confidence is Low unless a recognized direct error diagnostic supports a bounded explanation, in which case Medium is the maximum. The policy never emits High cause confidence. Missing-variable failures preserve the variable name; memory/authentication/DNS diagnostics retain their reported condition while stating that deeper causes remain unknown. Other hypotheses are replaced with insufficiency rather than inventing causal truth.
- Model summary, noise assessment, cause, missing-information and action prose is replaced by bounded source-derived statements and canonical diagnostic guidance. This prevents unsupported recovery/causal assertions from surviving in an adjacent text field. It intentionally reduces free-form specificity.
- Candidate recommendations, steps and verification steps must satisfy a closed read-only diagnostic grammar. Unknown syntax, shell commands, database deletion, tool invocations and mutations trigger `humanReviewRequired`. Candidate commands are removed; the result explicitly requires owner approval, verified backups and rollback planning, and warns about data loss. Verification contains only canonical read-only guidance. There is **no command execution mechanism** or automatic approval path.
- `safetyReview` records policy version, decision basis, review reasons and supporting line numbers in existing decision JSONB. The normal recommendation text also carries the human-review requirement, so protection does not depend on a new frontend feature.

## Historical replay and regressions

[AI_ENGINE_STORED_REPLAY.json](AI_ENGINE_STORED_REPLAY.json) records an offline transformation of nine returned October 7-directory responses using current fixture text. It made zero provider calls or database writes. Eight satisfy the current rubric; the stored injection case becomes **Medium/Investigate** and fails its unchanged **Low-only** severity expectation. This conservative deviation is visible and was not hidden by relaxing that bound. All nine returned causes become Low/Medium instead of retaining unsupported High labels. This is policy behavior on stored synthetic outputs, not improved live accuracy, a replacement for the original 4/11 result, or an independent confidence evaluation.

No failing automated regression remains. Known behavior changes include more investigations and human-review warnings, no High causal confidence, and less free-form cause/action detail. All nine historical responses require review because their free-form action text lies outside the closed diagnostic grammar. This conservatism may reduce diagnostic usefulness and needs independent human evaluation before relaxing policy.

The safe generation path does **not** backfill history or revalidate completed records returned by old idempotency replays. Old assessments can still contain pre-policy decisions and advice; policy metadata distinguishes newly generated results. No production database rewrite was authorized or attempted. Any history presentation/revalidation change must preserve account isolation, replay semantics and severity filtering coherently.

## Remaining work and readiness

1. Extend grounded diagnostic/recovery coverage using independently adjudicated examples without weakening conservative handling. Validate real security/data-loss cases, mixed service streams, long excerpts, JSON logs and timestamp variants; current Critical rules are request-counter based and do not claim complete incident coverage.
2. Review the intentional usefulness/false-positive cost of constrained prose, action grammar and the injection severity deviation. Any broader hypothesis support needs a defendable evidence policy rather than simply accepting model confidence again.
3. Design safe handling of historical records and completed replays; this change applies to new generation and leaves stored legacy results untouched.
4. Resolve provider project capacity and conduct a separately approved, budgeted live evaluation for availability, latency, long-output completion, false negatives, false positives, causal usefulness and confidence calibration. Do not interpret offline passes as measured diagnostic accuracy.
5. Retain human verification and prohibit automated alert suppression/remediation based only on these decisions. Do not silently switch providers or models.
